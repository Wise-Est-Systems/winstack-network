Honest notes about how this works

Free during launch. Sealing is free while we're building this out. We'll add a paid option later for unlimited sealing and for the hardware witness device — but every seal made during launch stays valid forever, no matter what happens to pricing.

Verification is free forever, by design. Anyone you send a .win to can verify it on winstack.dev with no account, no payment, no contact with us. That part of the protocol is open source and will never be paywalled. It works even if we disappear.

The witness, today, is Wise.Est Systems. We sign your seal with our Ed25519 key — proving a real third party received your file at a specific moment, the role a notary plays for paper documents. Our public key is pinned in the verifier and published openly.

A hardware witness is coming. A small device that holds your own witness key — so you don't have to depend on us being the witness at all. Once it's out, your seals are signed by your own hand, not ours. The protocol is built so this transition just works.

Read the protocol. Specs and source at github.com/Wise-Est-Systems. Built to be implementable from scratch by anyone — so it survives us, and the people we love after us.
