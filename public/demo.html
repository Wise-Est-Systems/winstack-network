What this proof means — and what it doesn't

A verified .win proves

  • The answer is unchanged since it was sealed
  • The sealing key signed this exact proof
  • The proof travels inside the file — verify offline, no account

It does NOT prove

  • That the answer is factually true
  • Who the real-world signer is
  • That it was the first copy ever made
